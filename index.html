Privacy

This site has no tracking, cookies, or analytics. The "Request a Repair" form doesn't send anything to a server — it opens your own email app with your details filled in, and it's up to you to hit send. Nothing you type is stored or seen by anyone until you send that email yourself. Information you send by email, text, or phone is used only to schedule and complete your repair.

Accessibility

This site is built with plain HTML, real form labels, and visible focus states so it works with screen readers and keyboard navigation. Text and buttons scale with your device's font settings.
